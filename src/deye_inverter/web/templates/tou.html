Charging settings in the inverter

Grid charge is the inverter's master switch: the Grid charge boxes in the Time of Use table below charge from the grid only while it is on, and only up to the slot's SOC. Grid charge current limits how fast the grid charges the battery. Values are read from the inverter every 30 minutes and after every change.
